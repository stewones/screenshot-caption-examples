App Store screenshot caption examples by app category

Short headlines that work on App Store and Google Play screenshots, grouped by category. Use them as starting points: swap in your own outcome and keep each one under about seven words.

The pattern behind almost all of them: verb + outcome + (optional) qualifier. "Track every expense in seconds" = track (verb) + every expense (outcome) + in seconds (qualifier).

How to pick and test yours

  • First frame = the main outcome. Save feature names and secondary perks for frames three to five.
  • Read it at thumbnail size. If it needs a second line on a phone held at arm's length, cut words.
  • One idea per frame. A caption with "and" in it is usually two screenshots.
  • Proof beats adjectives. "Rated 4.8 by 20,000 runners" says more than "The best running app".
  • Test one change at a time. Use App Store Product Page Optimization or Google Play store listing experiments and change only the first caption.
